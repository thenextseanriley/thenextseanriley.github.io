Health policy · implementation science

Sean R. Riley

PhD candidate and health services researcher at The Ohio State University

My research examines what happens between a policy’s passage and the care patients receive: how policies enable or constrain care delivery, what organizations need to implement them, and whether the benefits justify the costs. My current work examines the costs and demands of substance-use care in primary care. Building on that work and my research in advance care planning and assisted-dying oversight, I am developing a program on how end-of-life policies help patients’ preferences become usable in clinical care.
